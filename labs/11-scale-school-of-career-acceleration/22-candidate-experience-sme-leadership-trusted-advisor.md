# Candidate Experience / RMK — Step 22: SME Leadership & Trusted Advisor

## Objective

Demonstrate senior-level Candidate Experience / Recruiting Marketing advisory capability across Career Site Builder, CRM, analytics, governance and transformation.

## Scenario Practice

- Executive leadership asks whether to standardize global career-site experience.
- Marketing and Talent Acquisition disagree on employer-brand ownership.
- CRM adoption is low despite technically complete configuration.
- A client wants a future-state Candidate Experience roadmap.

## Interview Focus

Answer as an advisor: **business outcome → candidate journey → product capability → operating model → governance → measurement → roadmap**.

## Evidence

Use genuine examples of design workshops, executive communication, mentoring, decision records, adoption plans and measurable improvements.

## Rapid-Fire

1. What makes a Candidate Experience SME different from a configurator?
2. How do you balance brand flexibility with platform governance?
3. How do you establish CRM operating ownership?
4. How do you measure candidate experience?
5. How do you build a 12–24 month roadmap?

**Master loop:** UNDERSTAND → ARCHITECT → ENABLE → ADOPT → MEASURE → TRANSFORM
