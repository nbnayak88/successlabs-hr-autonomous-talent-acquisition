# Scenario Category 13 — Production Incident, Recovery & Hypercare

## Objective
Prepare for live incidents affecting candidate experience.

## 10 Scenario Themes
1. Site unavailable
2. Jobs disappear
3. Incorrect job content
4. Application-link failure
5. Subscription failure
6. Campaign tracking failure
7. Localization defect
8. Accessibility regression
9. Domain/SSL incident
10. Performance degradation

## Incident Framework
**SYMPTOM → IMPACT → CONTAIN → EVIDENCE → ISOLATE → RECOVER → VALIDATE → COMMUNICATE → RCA → PREVENT**
