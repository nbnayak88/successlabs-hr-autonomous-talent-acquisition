# Scenario Category 12 — Candidate Experience Data Quality & Reconciliation

## Objective
Establish measurable data quality across Recruiting, Candidate Experience, CRM and analytics.

## Quality Dimensions
Completeness, accuracy, consistency, validity, timeliness, uniqueness, referential integrity.

## 10 Scenarios
1. Job population mismatch
2. Incorrect job fields
3. Candidate subscription data
4. CRM/talent-pool data
5. Analytics reconciliation
6. Duplicate campaign sources
7. Localization mismatch
8. Historical information
9. Mass organizational/job-taxonomy change
10. Data-quality governance

## Master Framework
**DEFINE → MEASURE → DETECT → TRACE → REMEDIATE → RECONCILE → PREVENT → GOVERN**
