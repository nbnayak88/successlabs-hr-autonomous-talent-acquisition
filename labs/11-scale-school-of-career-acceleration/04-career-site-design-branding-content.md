# Scenario Category 04 — Career Site Design, Branding & Content

## Objective
Prepare for employer-branding, content architecture and candidate-UX scenarios.

## 10 Scenario Themes
1. Global brand with local variation
2. Employer-value-proposition storytelling
3. Mobile-first career experience
4. Accessibility remediation
5. Content governance
6. Media-heavy page performance
7. Search-first job discovery
8. Privacy/legal content
9. Conversion-oriented redesign
10. Executive brand refresh

## Strong Answer Pattern
**Candidate Need → Content Goal → Information Architecture → Component Choice → Accessibility → Localization → Performance → Measurement**

## Interview Scenario
Marketing wants video-heavy career pages. Explain how you balance storytelling with page performance, mobile behavior, accessibility, content ownership and measurable candidate engagement.

## Anti-Patterns
- Desktop-only design
- Accessibility as an afterthought
- Local duplication of global content
- Content without owners
- Measuring only page views
