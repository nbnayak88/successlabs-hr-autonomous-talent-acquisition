# Candidate Experience / RMK — Step 21: Recruiting Transformation & Continuous Improvement

## Objective

Turn Candidate Experience capabilities into measurable improvement across attraction, engagement, conversion and journey performance.

## Scenario Practice

- Career-site traffic is strong but application conversion is weak.
- Talent-pool campaigns generate engagement but little conversion.
- A global career site needs continuous optimization without uncontrolled local changes.
- Source analytics reveal recurring drop-off at a specific journey stage.

## Interview Focus

Connect **candidate journey → data → hypothesis → experiment → measurement → improvement**.

## Evidence

Prepare genuine examples of experiments, source/campaign analytics, candidate feedback, accessibility improvements, content governance and measurable conversion outcomes.

## Rapid-Fire

1. Which journey metric would you establish first?
2. How do you distinguish content, UX and process problems?
3. How do you prioritize improvements?
4. How do you protect global standards?
5. What proves the improvement worked?

**Master loop:** ATTRACT → ENGAGE → CONVERT → MEASURE → EXPERIMENT → IMPROVE
