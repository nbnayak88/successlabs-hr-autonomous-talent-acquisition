# Lovable × SAP SuccessFactors × SmartRecruiters
# Real Estate Recruiting Transformation — “The Shortlist”

## 1. Build Brief

Create a **cinematic, interactive recruiting transformation simulation** for a fictional real-estate enterprise:

> **OmniSpace Real Estate Ltd.**

The experience should demonstrate how a modern real-estate organization can transform recruiting using **SAP SuccessFactors Recruiting** and **SmartRecruiters-inspired talent acquisition capabilities**.

This is a **learning/demo case study**, not a production recruiting platform.

The simulation should connect:

**WORKFORCE DEMAND → REQUISITION → ATTRACT → SCREEN → SHORTLIST → INTERVIEW → DECIDE → OFFER → HIRE → ONBOARD**

The application should feel like a **digital transformation case study brought to life**, rather than a collection of CRUD screens.

---

# 2. Case Study Context

## Company

**OmniSpace Real Estate Ltd.**

A global real-estate enterprise operating across:

- Commercial real estate
- Residential communities
- Property management
- Smart buildings
- Facilities management
- Real-estate technology
- ESG and sustainability
- Asset management
- Development projects

## Transformation challenge

OmniSpace is expanding its portfolio of **smart buildings, ESG-led developments, digital property services, and data-driven operations**.

Recruiting currently suffers from:

- slow demand-to-requisition cycles,
- inconsistent requisitions,
- fragmented sourcing,
- inconsistent candidate screening,
- manual interview coordination,
- delayed hiring-manager decisions,
- disconnected offer approvals,
- limited funnel visibility,
- poor handoff into onboarding.

## Transformation vision

> **Build a fast, intelligent, and experience-led recruiting ecosystem that attracts the right talent, reduces hiring friction, and gives leaders trusted visibility into workforce demand.**

## Scoring model

Use transparent rule-based simulation.

### Weighting

- Real-estate / facilities experience: 30%
- Smart-building / digital skills: 25%
- Availability: 15%
- Distance to property: 10%
- Shift alignment: 10%
- Tenant/customer service: 10%

Total = 100%.

### Score formula

```text
Shortlist Score =
Facilities Experience × 0.30
+ Smart Building Skills × 0.25
+ Availability × 0.15
+ Distance × 0.10
+ Shift Match × 0.10
+ Tenant Service × 0.10
```

Display evidence behind the score.

# 42. Demo Guardrails

1. This is a fictional learning simulation.
2. No production candidate data.
3. No real recruiting transactions.
4. No real SmartRecruiters API integration.
5. No real SAP SuccessFactors API integration.
6. AI is a deterministic demo scoring model.
7. HRIS export is simulated.
8. Candidate decisions remain human-controlled.
9. Do not copy proprietary product screens pixel-for-pixel.
10. Use original OmniSpace visual identity layered with SmartRecruiters-inspired green.
11. Avoid real-person PII.
12. Clearly label simulated AI and simulated integrations.

# 45. Product Experience Principle

Do not build disconnected recruiting pages.

Build a **living real-estate recruiting story**.

The learner should always know:

**WHERE AM I?**

**WHO AM I ACTING AS?**

**WHAT BUSINESS OUTCOME IS AT STAKE?**

**WHAT EVIDENCE DO I HAVE?**

**WHAT DECISION AM I MAKING?**

**WHAT HAPPENS NEXT?**

The central narrative is:

> **A building cannot become operational until the right people are hired.**

The recruiting platform is therefore not simply processing candidates.

It is **converting workforce strategy into operational capability**.
