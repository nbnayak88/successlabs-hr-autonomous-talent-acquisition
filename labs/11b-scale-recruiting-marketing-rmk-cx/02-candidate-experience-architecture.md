# Scenario Category 02 — Candidate Experience Architecture

## Objective
Demonstrate architectural understanding of the Candidate Experience landscape.

Candidate Experience is best understood as an experience ecosystem: Career Site Builder provides the candidate-facing content/experience layer; Recruiting provides job and application context; Candidate Relationship Management supports engagement such as talent pools, candidate data capture and email campaigns; Advanced Analytics measures source and funnel performance.

## Interview Scenarios
1. Explain the end-to-end Candidate Experience architecture.
2. Consolidate fragmented regional career sites.
3. Diagnose an analytics/source mismatch.
4. Explain Candidate Experience vs Recruiting boundaries.
5. Position CRM within the candidate journey.
6. Connect job metadata to search and filters.
7. Design security/privacy controls.
8. Design global/local localization.
9. Design domain, SSL and release readiness.
10. Act as architecture SME.

## Rapid-Fire
- Where does CSB fit?
- Where does CRM fit?
- Where does Advanced Analytics fit?
- Why should tracking be designed before campaign launch?
- What are the major architecture risks?
