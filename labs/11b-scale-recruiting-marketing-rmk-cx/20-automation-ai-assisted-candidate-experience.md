# Scenario Category 20 — Automation, AI & Intelligent Candidate Experience

## Objective
Prepare for applied AI and automation questions without turning the role into an AI-engineering role.

## 10 Use Cases
1. Requirement summarization
2. Content drafting
3. Accessibility-content checks
4. Search/content classification
5. Campaign-content variants
6. Candidate-journey analysis
7. Analytics anomaly detection
8. Defect clustering
9. Test-scenario generation
10. Knowledge management

## AI Governance
**BUSINESS PROBLEM → DATA → AI ASSISTANCE → HUMAN REVIEW → GOVERNANCE → MEASURE → SCALE**

## Anti-Patterns
- Treating AI output as authoritative
- Using candidate PII in unapproved tools
- Automating high-impact candidate decisions without governance
- Publishing AI-generated content without review
- Measuring AI activity rather than business value
