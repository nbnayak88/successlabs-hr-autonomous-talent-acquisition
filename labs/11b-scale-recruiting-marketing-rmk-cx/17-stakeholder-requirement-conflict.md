# Scenario Category 17 — Stakeholder Requirement Conflict & Experience Design

## Objective
Handle conflict among HR, Recruiting, Marketing, IT, Legal, Security, Accessibility and regional teams.

## 10 Scenarios
1. Marketing vs Recruiting
2. Global vs local
3. Design vs accessibility
4. Candidate speed vs data capture
5. Engagement vs privacy
6. Analytics vs simplicity
7. Branding vs performance
8. Standardization vs customization
9. Governance vs local autonomy
10. Business urgency vs release quality

## Strong Answer
**CLARIFY → SEPARATE NON-NEGOTIABLES → DEFINE DECISION CRITERIA → PRESENT OPTIONS → AGREE → DOCUMENT → VALIDATE**
