## Q01 — How would you map the annual compensation process?

### Interview Question
How would you explain the end-to-end annual compensation process to an interviewer?

### STAR Answer
**Situation:** The organization had fragmented activities across HR, managers, finance, and payroll.

**Task:** I needed to create a common process view.

**Action:** I mapped policy and planning preparation, employee eligibility, budget allocation, manager recommendations, review and approval, finalization, employee communication, downstream processing, and post-cycle analysis.

**Result:** Every stakeholder could understand where their responsibility began and ended.

### SAP SuccessFactors Compensation & Variable Pay Example
The SuccessFactors cycle can support planning from eligible employee data through manager worksheets, approvals, finalized compensation, and statements.

### SME Probe

## Q02 — How does compensation philosophy influence system design?

### Interview Question
Why should compensation philosophy be understood before configuring a compensation system?

### STAR Answer
**Situation:** A client wanted to configure the system before agreeing how compensation decisions should work.

**Task:** I needed to prevent technology from defining business policy accidentally.

**Action:** I clarified principles around pay positioning, performance differentiation, market competitiveness, equity, affordability, and managerial discretion before translating them into process rules.

**Result:** The technology supported the intended compensation philosophy rather than creating one by default.

### SAP SuccessFactors Compensation & Variable Pay Example
Guidelines, budgets, eligibility, and worksheet behavior should reflect approved compensation policy.

### SME Probe
What happens when system behavior conflicts with compensation philosophy?

**Situation:** The planning cycle could not safely proceed with unreliable employee data.

**Task:** I needed to protect data quality without losing the business deadline unnecessarily.

**Action:** I assessed affected populations, identified the source-data issue, established correction ownership, defined validation criteria, and evaluated whether unaffected populations could proceed safely.

**Result:** The organization avoided making incorrect compensation decisions while maintaining controlled progress.

### SAP SuccessFactors Compensation & Variable Pay Example
