# ARP5 — Theme 17: Stakeholder Management

## 20 Scenario-Based Interview Questions & STAR Answers

**Stream:** ARP5 — Applied SAP SuccessFactors Compensation & Variable Pay  
**Theme:** 17 — Stakeholder Management  
**Target:** 20 unique scenarios  
**Answer method:** STAR — Situation → Task → Action → Result  
**Product posture:** HCM-first, consultative, outcome-oriented

> **Boundary:** Theme 17 focuses on aligning HR, Finance, managers, executives, Payroll, IT, Security, and other stakeholders around Compensation decisions. It is distinct from Theme 18 Communication & Consulting, which focuses on communication techniques and consulting behaviors.

### Stakeholder Management Spine

**Identify → Understand → Align → Prioritize → Decide → Govern → Execute → Validate → Sustain**

## Q01 — HR and Finance Have Different Priorities

### Interview Question
How would you manage a Compensation program where HR prioritizes employee experience and Finance prioritizes cost control?

### STAR Answer
**Situation:** HR wanted flexibility for managers while Finance wanted strict budget discipline.  
**Task:** I needed to align both functions around a common solution.  
**Action:** I translated both positions into measurable outcomes, identified non-negotiable controls, evaluated solution options, and made trade-offs explicit through joint governance.  
**Result:** Both functions agreed on a Compensation design that balanced manager flexibility with financial control.

### SAP SuccessFactors Compensation & Variable Pay Example
Use budgets, guidelines, approval workflows, and controlled exceptions to balance reward decisions with financial governance.

### SME Probe
How do you prevent one stakeholder from dominating the architecture?

## Q02 — Executive Sponsor Wants Speed

### Interview Question
An executive sponsor wants the Compensation solution delivered faster than the project team considers realistic. What would you do?

### STAR Answer
**Situation:** Leadership requested an accelerated implementation timeline.  
**Task:** I needed to protect the critical business date without creating unacceptable solution risk.  
**Action:** I separated must-have capabilities from later enhancements, mapped dependencies and risks, created a phased delivery option, and made the trade-offs visible to the sponsor.  
**Result:** Leadership received a realistic path to the target date without hiding delivery risk.

### SAP SuccessFactors Compensation & Variable Pay Example
Prioritize core Compensation cycle capabilities while sequencing advanced reporting, enhancements, or non-critical experience improvements.

### SME Probe

## Q04 — Manager Resistance to Standardization

### Interview Question

### STAR Answer
**Situation:** Managers perceived standardized guidelines and workflows as restrictions.  
**Task:** I needed to preserve appropriate decision autonomy while maintaining governance.

**Result:** Managers understood the boundaries and adoption improved.

### SAP SuccessFactors Compensation & Variable Pay Example

**Situation:** Regional HR teams requested different approaches to the same compensation process.  
**Task:** I needed to create a scalable global decision.  
**Action:** I classified each requirement as global policy, legal/regulatory necessity, business practice, or preference; then prioritized mandatory differences and standardized the rest.

## Q07 — Finance Questions Compensation Calculations

### Interview Question

### STAR Answer
**Situation:** Finance questioned the configured bonus calculation.  
**Task:** I needed to establish a shared understanding of the business rule.
