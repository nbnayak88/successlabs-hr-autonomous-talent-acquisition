## Q01 — Discovering the Real Client Need

### Interview Question
A prospect asks for a SuccessFactors Compensation demo. How would you determine what they actually need before demonstrating the product?

### STAR Answer
**Situation:** A prospect requested a generic Compensation demonstration.  
**Task:** I needed to understand the business problem before positioning a solution.  
**Action:** I asked about compensation philosophy, annual-cycle pain points, workforce populations, manager experience, budget governance, integrations, reporting, controls, and desired outcomes.  
**Result:** The demonstration became specific to the client's business priorities rather than a feature tour.

### SAP SuccessFactors Compensation & Variable Pay Example
Demonstrate worksheets, guidelines, budgets, workflow, Variable Pay, statements, and integrations only against identified business scenarios.
