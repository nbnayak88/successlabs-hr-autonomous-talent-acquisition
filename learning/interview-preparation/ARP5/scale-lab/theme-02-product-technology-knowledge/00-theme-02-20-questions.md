## Q01 — How would you explain SAP SuccessFactors Compensation?

### STAR Answer
**Situation:** A global organization managed annual compensation through spreadsheets and disconnected processes.

**Task:** I needed to explain how a dedicated compensation platform could improve the process.

**Action:** I described how SuccessFactors Compensation can structure eligibility, budgets, guidelines, manager planning, calculations, approvals, and employee communication within a governed cycle.

**Result:** The organization could move toward a controlled, repeatable compensation-planning process.
