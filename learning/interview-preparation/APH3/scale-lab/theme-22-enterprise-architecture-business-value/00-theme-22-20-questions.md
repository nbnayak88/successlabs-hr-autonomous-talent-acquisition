### Interview Question

### STAR Answer
**Situation:** Performance management was treated as an isolated HR application.

**Action:** I mapped strategic objectives to organizational goals, individual performance, workforce capability, decision-making, talent outcomes, and business results. I then mapped the supporting processes, data, applications, integrations, security, and experiences.

**Result:** Performance & Goals became part of the enterprise workforce architecture rather than a standalone application.
