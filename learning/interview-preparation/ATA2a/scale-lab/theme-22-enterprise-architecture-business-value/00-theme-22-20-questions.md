## HR-ATA2A-B22-Q01 — Enterprise Recruiting Architecture

### Interview Question
How would you position SmartRecruiters within an enterprise recruiting architecture?

### STAR Answer
**Situation:** A global organization was treating its ATS as an isolated application.

**Task:** I needed to establish a coherent enterprise architecture for recruiting.

**Action:** I mapped business capabilities, recruit-to-select processes, data ownership, applications, integrations, identity, security, experience, analytics, and operating responsibilities.

**Result:** SmartRecruiters became a clearly bounded recruiting capability within a connected enterprise architecture.

### SmartRecruiters Example
SmartRecruiters would own defined recruiting capabilities while integrating with sourcing, assessment, HCM, identity, analytics, and other enterprise services.

### SME Probe
What should determine the boundary of an enterprise application?

## HR-ATA2A-B22-Q04 — Value Stream Architecture

### Interview Question

### STAR Answer
**Situation:** Recruiting teams optimized individual activities but the end-to-end candidate journey remained slow.

**Task:** I needed to optimize the complete value flow.

**Action:** I mapped candidate value from requisition through sourcing, screening, assessment, interview, selection, offer, and hire, then identified delays, handoffs, rework, and unnecessary controls.

**Result:** Architecture improvements targeted end-to-end value rather than isolated system efficiency.
