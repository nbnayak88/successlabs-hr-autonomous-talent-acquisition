## HR-ATA2A-B21-Q01 — Evaluating Emerging Technology

### Interview Question
How would you evaluate an emerging technology for a SmartRecruiters recruiting transformation?

### STAR Answer
**Situation:** Leadership proposed adopting a new technology to improve recruiting.

**Task:** I needed to determine whether it solved a real business problem.

**Action:** I evaluated the use case, maturity, business value, data requirements, integration impact, security, candidate experience, scalability, cost, vendor viability, and reversibility.

**Result:** The organization made an evidence-based innovation decision rather than adopting technology because it was fashionable.

### SmartRecruiters Example
I would evaluate any emerging capability against a specific SmartRecruiters recruiting outcome before adding it to the architecture.

### SME Probe
What makes an emerging technology worth piloting?

## HR-ATA2A-B21-Q02 — Generative AI in Recruiting

### Interview Question
How would you introduce generative AI into recruiting without creating uncontrolled risk?

### STAR Answer
**Situation:** Recruiters wanted generative AI assistance for job content and candidate interactions.

**Task:** I needed to enable productivity while protecting candidate trust and decision quality.

**Action:** I started with bounded use cases, defined human oversight, data boundaries, privacy controls, prompt/data governance, quality checks, monitoring, and approval criteria.

**Result:** AI was introduced as controlled assistance rather than unrestricted automation.

### SmartRecruiters Example
Generative AI could assist with approved recruiting content or recruiter productivity while consequential candidate decisions remain governed.

### SME Probe
Which recruiting AI use cases should require the strongest human oversight?

# Theme 21 Completion Standard

A learner completes **ATA2a Theme 21 — Innovation & Emerging Technology** when they can:

- Evaluate emerging technology against business outcomes.
- Introduce generative AI responsibly.
- Assess AI matching, sourcing, analytics, and agents.
- Establish responsible-AI governance.
- Explore skills intelligence and talent ecosystems.
- Evaluate emerging identity and assessment technologies.
- Build controlled innovation sandboxes.
- Maintain a recruiting technology radar.
- Challenge vendor innovation claims with evidence.
- Design future-ready recruiting architecture.
- Move innovation from experiment to governed enterprise capability.
- Balance innovation, candidate experience, security, privacy, fairness, and business value.

**Quality rule:** Every scenario must demonstrate Situation → Task → Action → Result, contain a distinct innovation/emerging-technology decision, use **SmartRecruiters** as the primary platform example, remain separate from onboarding, avoid duplication with Themes 01–20, and end with an SME Probe.

**Scenario IDs:** HR-ATA2A-B21-Q01 → HR-ATA2A-B21-Q20

**Target achieved:** 20 unique scenario-based interview questions + 20 STAR answers.
