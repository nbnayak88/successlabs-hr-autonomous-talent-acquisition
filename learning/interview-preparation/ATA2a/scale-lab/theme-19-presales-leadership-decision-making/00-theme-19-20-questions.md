## HR-ATA2A-B19-Q01 — Executive Presales Discovery

### Interview Question
How would you lead a presales discovery conversation for a client considering SmartRecruiters?

### STAR Answer
**Situation:** A global organization wanted to modernize its legacy ATS but had not defined its target recruiting architecture.

**Task:** I needed to understand the transformation opportunity before recommending a solution.

**Action:** I explored business strategy, recruiting maturity, process pain points, candidate experience, data, integrations, security, operating model, adoption, and measurable outcomes.

**Result:** The client received an evidence-based transformation hypothesis instead of a generic product pitch.

### SmartRecruiters Example
I would position SmartRecruiters within the recruit-to-select ecosystem and assess fit against the client's business and architecture requirements.

### SME Probe
What should you discover before demonstrating product features?

## HR-ATA2A-B19-Q07 — Leadership During Crisis

### Interview Question

### STAR Answer
**Situation:** A production incident threatened a high-priority recruitment campaign.

**Task:** I needed to stabilize operations and provide leadership under pressure.

**Action:** I established incident command, clarified roles, prioritized business-critical recruiting journeys, separated containment from root-cause remediation, and communicated decisions and risks.

### SmartRecruiters Example

## HR-ATA2A-B19-Q15 — Leadership and Risk Acceptance

### Interview Question
A business sponsor wants to accept a significant recruiting risk to meet a deadline. What would you do?

### STAR Answer
**Situation:** A release deadline conflicted with an unresolved recruiting risk.

**Task:** I needed to ensure the decision was informed and properly governed.

**Action:** I quantified impact, likelihood, affected users, controls, mitigation, contingency, and residual risk, then presented the decision to the authorized risk owner.

**Result:** The risk was either mitigated or explicitly accepted by the accountable authority.

### SmartRecruiters Example
A SmartRecruiters go-live risk involving candidate data, critical integrations, or security should never be silently accepted by the project team.

### SME Probe
Who has the authority to accept enterprise risk?
