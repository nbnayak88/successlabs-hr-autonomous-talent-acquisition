## HR-AGL4-B05-Q01 — Discovering the Real Succession Requirement

### Interview Question
A client asks, “We need a succession module.” How would you discover the actual business requirement?

### STAR Answer
**Situation:** The request was framed as a technology purchase without a clearly defined business problem.

**Task:** I needed to uncover the outcome the organization actually wanted.

**Action:** I asked about critical roles, business continuity risk, successor coverage, readiness, talent gaps, development, career mobility, governance, and decision-making. I translated those needs into business capabilities before discussing configuration.

**Result:** The requirement changed from “implement succession software” to a measurable capability for protecting critical-role continuity.

### SAP SuccessFactors Succession & Development Example
I would map the requirement to critical positions, successors, readiness, talent profiles, development, career, and succession analytics before defining the SuccessFactors solution scope.

### SME Probe
What question would you ask first when a stakeholder gives you a technology-led requirement?

## HR-AGL4-B05-Q02 — Critical-Role Requirement

### Interview Question
Business leaders cannot agree on what makes a position critical. How would you resolve the requirement?

### STAR Answer
**Situation:** Different executives used different definitions of criticality.

**Task:** I needed to establish an objective requirement.

**Action:** I facilitated workshops around strategic impact, operational dependency, scarce capability, replacement difficulty, regulatory exposure, and time-to-competence. I converted the discussion into agreed criteria and governance.

**Result:** Critical-role identification became a repeatable business rule rather than executive opinion.

### SAP SuccessFactors Succession & Development Example
The agreed criteria can be used to identify and govern critical positions within succession planning.

### SME Probe
Who should approve the enterprise definition of a critical position?

## HR-AGL4-B05-Q04 — Manager Requirement versus Employee Requirement

### Interview Question
Managers want employees nominated for succession, while employees want control over their career direction. How would you capture both requirements?

### STAR Answer

**Task:** I needed to represent both perspectives without treating them as equivalent decisions.

**Action:** I separated manager succession nomination from employee career aspiration. I included both in discovery and defined where each could influence the talent process.

**Result:** The solution respected business continuity needs while creating a more employee-centered career experience.

### SAP SuccessFactors Succession & Development Example

### Interview Question

**Task:** I needed to establish a defensible delivery sequence.

### SAP SuccessFactors Succession & Development Example

### Interview Question

### STAR Answer
**Situation:** The phrase “real-time” had no agreed business meaning.

**Task:** I needed to determine the actual information freshness requirement.

**Action:** I asked what decision depends on the data, acceptable latency, source update frequency, critical metrics, and reconciliation expectations. I then defined the required reporting freshness based on business value.

**Result:** The organization avoided unnecessary technical complexity while meeting the real decision need.

### SAP SuccessFactors Succession & Development Example
