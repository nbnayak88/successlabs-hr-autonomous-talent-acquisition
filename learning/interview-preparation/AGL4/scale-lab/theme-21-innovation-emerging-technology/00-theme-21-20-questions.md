### HR-AGL4-B21-Q01 — Evaluating an Emerging Technology

**Interview Question:** How would you decide whether an emerging HR technology belongs on a Succession & Development roadmap?

### STAR Answer
**Situation:** Leadership wanted to adopt a new talent technology because it was receiving significant market attention.

**Task:** I needed to determine whether it solved a meaningful business problem.

**Action:** I evaluated strategic fit, business value, data readiness, architecture fit, security, adoption, maturity, cost and measurable outcomes before recommending a controlled experiment.

**Result:** The organization avoided technology-led experimentation and focused innovation on a real workforce capability gap.

**SAP SuccessFactors Succession & Development Example:** I would assess whether an AI-enabled talent insight capability improves succession coverage, readiness or workforce decisions before scaling it.

**SME Probe:** What criteria distinguish useful innovation from technology hype?

### HR-AGL4-B21-Q02 — AI-Assisted Succession Recommendations

**Interview Question:** How would you introduce AI-assisted successor recommendations responsibly?

### STAR Answer
**Situation:** HR wanted to use AI to identify potential successors for critical roles.

**Task:** I needed to improve decision support without turning talent decisions into an uncontrolled automated process.

**Action:** I established trusted data requirements, explainability expectations, human review, bias monitoring, privacy controls and measurable pilot outcomes.

**Result:** HR could use AI as decision support while retaining accountable human judgment.

**SAP SuccessFactors Succession & Development Example:** AI could surface potential successor patterns from governed talent information, while HR leaders validate recommendations before decisions.

**SME Probe:** What decisions should remain explicitly human-controlled?

---

### HR-AGL4-B21-Q03 — AI Bias in Talent Decisions

**Interview Question:** What would you do if an AI talent recommendation appeared biased?

### STAR Answer
**Situation:** An AI-assisted talent analysis produced recommendations that disproportionately favored a particular employee population.

**Task:** I needed to protect fairness and determine whether the issue originated in data, model behavior or process design.

**Action:** I paused affected decision automation, investigated data lineage and model behavior, involved HR/legal/security stakeholders, tested for bias and established remediation controls.

**Result:** The organization prevented potentially harmful talent decisions and strengthened responsible-AI governance.

**SAP SuccessFactors Succession & Development Example:** AI recommendations would remain subject to governance, human review and appropriate privacy and fairness controls.

**SME Probe:** How would you distinguish model bias from biased source data?

### HR-AGL4-B21-Q04 — Talent Marketplace and Career Mobility

**Interview Question:** How could an internal talent marketplace change Succession & Development?

### STAR Answer
**Situation:** The organization had succession plans but employees struggled to discover internal career opportunities.

**Task:** I needed to connect succession with broader workforce mobility.

**Action:** I linked talent profiles, skills, aspirations, development opportunities, career paths and critical-role requirements into a mobility-oriented capability model.

**Result:** Succession evolved from replacement planning toward continuous internal talent movement and capability development.

**SAP SuccessFactors Succession & Development Example:** Career development and talent visibility can support movement from successor identification toward broader workforce capability.

**SME Probe:** How does talent mobility complement succession planning?

### HR-AGL4-B21-Q05 — Skills-Based Talent Architecture

**Interview Question:** How would you introduce skills-based architecture into a traditional succession model?

### STAR Answer
**Situation:** Succession was organized primarily around positions and employee relationships.

**Task:** I needed to make workforce capability more visible.

**Action:** I introduced a skills and capability perspective, mapped critical-role requirements to capabilities, connected employee profiles to relevant skills and aligned development opportunities.

**Result:** The organization could identify capability gaps in addition to individual successor gaps.

**SAP SuccessFactors Succession & Development Example:** Talent profiles and development information could support a more skills-oriented view of readiness and career mobility.

**SME Probe:** Why is a skills model valuable when position-based succession already exists?

### HR-AGL4-B21-Q06 — Generative AI for Talent Insights

**Interview Question:** Where could generative AI create value in Succession & Development without making high-risk decisions autonomously?

### STAR Answer
**Situation:** HR wanted to use generative AI across talent processes.

**Task:** I needed to identify safe, high-value use cases.

**Action:** I prioritized summarization, natural-language insight exploration, preparation for talent reviews, development-content assistance and knowledge retrieval, while keeping consequential talent decisions under human control.

**Result:** HR gained productivity and better access to information without inappropriate automation of sensitive decisions.

**SAP SuccessFactors Succession & Development Example:** Generative AI could help leaders prepare for talent discussions while the final succession decision remains governed by authorized humans.

**SME Probe:** What makes a generative-AI use case lower risk?

### HR-AGL4-B21-Q07 — Agentic AI in HR

**Interview Question:** How would you evaluate an AI agent that can execute Succession & Development tasks?

### STAR Answer
**Situation:** A new AI-agent capability could execute selected HR workflows rather than only provide recommendations.

**Task:** I needed to determine where agentic automation was appropriate.

**Action:** I classified actions by risk, defined authorization boundaries, human approval points, auditability, exception handling and rollback requirements.

**Result:** The organization could experiment with agentic HR safely instead of granting unrestricted autonomous access.

**SAP SuccessFactors Succession & Development Example:** An agent might prepare development-plan recommendations or identify missing information, while sensitive talent decisions require explicit approval.

**SME Probe:** What additional controls are required when AI moves from recommendation to action?

### HR-AGL4-B21-Q08 — Predictive Succession Risk

**Interview Question:** How would predictive analytics improve succession risk management?

### STAR Answer
**Situation:** Leadership discovered critical roles with no ready successors only after annual talent reviews.

**Task:** I needed to make succession risk more proactive.

**Action:** I identified leading indicators such as readiness, successor coverage, role criticality, capability gaps and talent movement, then established predictive risk views with human validation.

**Result:** Leaders could identify emerging succession risk earlier and initiate mitigation actions.

**SAP SuccessFactors Succession & Development Example:** Analytics could combine succession coverage, readiness and talent information to prioritize leadership attention.

**SME Probe:** What is the danger of treating a predictive score as a fact?

### HR-AGL4-B21-Q10 — Emerging Technology Pilot

**Interview Question:** How would you design a pilot for an emerging talent technology?

### STAR Answer
**Situation:** The organization wanted to experiment with a new AI-enabled talent capability.

**Task:** I needed to create evidence before enterprise-scale investment.

**Action:** I defined a bounded use case, target users, baseline measures, success criteria, data requirements, security controls, human oversight and a clear stop/scale decision.

**Result:** The pilot produced objective evidence for the roadmap.

**SAP SuccessFactors Succession & Development Example:** A pilot could test whether AI-assisted talent insights improve the quality and speed of succession-review preparation.

**SME Probe:** What evidence would cause you to stop the pilot?

### HR-AGL4-B21-Q11 — Emerging Technology and Privacy

**Interview Question:** How would you evaluate privacy risks when introducing AI into talent management?

### STAR Answer
**Situation:** A proposed AI capability required processing sensitive employee information.

**Task:** I needed to determine whether the use case could be implemented responsibly.

**Action:** I assessed data minimization, purpose limitation, access, retention, processing boundaries, transparency, vendor controls and human oversight.

**Result:** The organization could decide whether the use case was acceptable before exposing sensitive talent information.

**SAP SuccessFactors Succession & Development Example:** Sensitive talent and succession information would be processed only for approved purposes with appropriate access controls.

**SME Probe:** Why should privacy be evaluated during innovation design rather than after implementation?

### HR-AGL4-B21-Q12 — Technology vs Business Problem

**Interview Question:** A stakeholder proposes a new AI tool but cannot clearly state the business problem. What would you do?

### STAR Answer
**Situation:** A stakeholder was enthusiastic about an emerging technology but had no measurable business outcome.

**Task:** I needed to convert technology enthusiasm into a meaningful business case.

**Action:** I asked what workforce problem exists today, who experiences it, what decision or process is affected, what outcome should improve and how success would be measured.

**Result:** Either a valid use case emerged or the technology was correctly deprioritized.

**SAP SuccessFactors Succession & Development Example:** The use case would need to improve a defined outcome such as succession coverage, readiness or development effectiveness.

**SME Probe:** What question do you ask first when someone brings you a new technology?
