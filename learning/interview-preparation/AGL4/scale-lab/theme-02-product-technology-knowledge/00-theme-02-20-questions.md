## HR-AGL4-B02-Q01 — Selecting the Right Succession Capabilities

### Interview Question
A client asks for a succession solution but cannot distinguish succession planning from performance, compensation, and learning. How would you decide which SuccessFactors capabilities are required?

### STAR Answer
**Situation:** The client wanted a single talent solution covering performance, rewards, learning, and succession without clear capability boundaries.

**Task:** I had to identify the minimum Succession & Development capabilities needed for the succession business outcome.

**Action:** I mapped the requirement to critical positions, talent profiles, successors, readiness, talent pools, development planning, career mobility, and succession analytics. I explicitly kept performance, compensation, and learning as connected capabilities rather than duplicating their ownership inside succession.

**Result:** The solution scope became clearer, reduced overlap, and established a clean capability boundary for the architecture.

### SAP SuccessFactors Succession & Development Example
I would position Succession & Development as the system capability for identifying talent, successors, readiness, and development actions required for future workforce continuity.

### SME Probe
How would you prevent capability overlap across Succession, Performance, Compensation, and Learning?

## HR-AGL4-B02-Q03 — Position-Based Succession Planning

### Interview Question
A global organization wants succession plans to remain stable even when incumbents change. What product capability would you consider?

### STAR Answer
**Situation:** Succession plans were tied informally to current employees rather than the roles the business needed to protect.

**Task:** I had to move the design toward position-based succession.

**Action:** I modeled critical positions as the enduring business objects and associated incumbents, successors, readiness, and talent information with those positions. I separated the role requirement from the person currently occupying it.

**Result:** The succession plan remained usable when incumbents moved, resigned, or changed roles.

### SAP SuccessFactors Succession & Development Example
Position-based succession planning allows the organization to plan for continuity of critical roles rather than treating succession as a static employee list.

### SME Probe
When would you prefer position-based planning over a person-centric talent list?
