## HR-AGL4-B07-Q01 — Configuring Critical Positions

### Interview Question
How would you configure a set of critical positions for succession planning?

### STAR Answer
**Situation:** The client had agreed on its critical-role population but had no governed digital representation.

**Task:** I needed to configure the target positions for succession management.

**Action:** I validated position ownership and organizational relationships, confirmed the critical-role criteria, configured the relevant succession attributes, and tested the resulting position population before enabling successor planning.

**Result:** The business had a governed foundation for succession coverage and risk analysis.

### SAP SuccessFactors Succession & Development Example
I would configure critical positions and their relevant succession information in SuccessFactors while keeping authoritative organizational and position data aligned with Employee Central.

### SME Probe
How would you prevent outdated positions from remaining in the critical-role population?

## HR-AGL4-B07-Q10 — Configuring Approval and Governance Controls

### Interview Question
A business wants every successor change reviewed by HR. How would you determine the right configuration?

### STAR Answer
**Situation:** The organization wanted stronger governance over sensitive succession changes.

**Task:** I needed to translate the governance policy into a workable product process.

**Action:** I assessed whether the desired approval was supported natively, whether it was necessary for every change, and whether governance could instead use controlled permissions, auditability, and periodic review.

**Result:** Governance was strengthened without creating unnecessary administrative bottlenecks.

### SAP SuccessFactors Succession & Development Example
I would use supported permissions, workflows where applicable, audit mechanisms, and governance controls rather than adding approvals simply because they sound safer.
