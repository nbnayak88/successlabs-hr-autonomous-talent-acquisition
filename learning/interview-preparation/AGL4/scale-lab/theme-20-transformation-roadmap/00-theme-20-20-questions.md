### HR-AGL4-B20-Q01 — From Process Improvement to Transformation

**Interview Question:** How would you determine whether a Succession & Development initiative is a transformation or simply a system implementation?

### STAR Answer
**Situation:** An organization described its SuccessFactors implementation as a transformation, but the scope focused mainly on replicating existing processes.

**Task:** I needed to establish whether the initiative was actually changing workforce capability.

**Action:** I assessed changes across strategy, operating model, process, people, data, experience, technology, governance and measurable business outcomes.

**Result:** The organization could distinguish technology deployment from genuine transformation and define the missing transformation outcomes.

**SAP SuccessFactors Succession & Development Example:** Transformation would connect critical roles, talent visibility, readiness, development, mobility, analytics and workforce continuity rather than simply configure succession screens.

**SME Probe:** What is the strongest evidence that HR technology has created transformation?
