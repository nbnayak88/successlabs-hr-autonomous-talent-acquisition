### HR-AGL4-B22-Q01 — Connecting Succession to Enterprise Strategy

**Interview Question:** How would you connect Succession & Development architecture to enterprise strategy?

### STAR Answer
**Situation:** The organization treated succession as an HR process rather than a business capability.

**Task:** I needed to demonstrate why succession architecture mattered to enterprise strategy.

**Action:** I mapped strategic objectives to critical roles, workforce capabilities, talent risks, succession coverage and development priorities, then translated those relationships into architecture requirements.

**Result:** Succession became visibly connected to business continuity, growth and workforce resilience.

**SAP SuccessFactors Succession & Development Example:** Critical-position management, talent profiles, successor readiness and development can be positioned as capabilities supporting strategic workforce outcomes.

**SME Probe:** What enterprise outcome makes succession strategically important?

### HR-AGL4-B22-Q05 — Integration Architecture

**Interview Question:** How would you architect integrations between Succession & Development and the wider HR ecosystem?

### STAR Answer
**Situation:** Succession depended on information from core HR, performance, learning and other systems.

**Task:** I needed to create reliable information flows without creating point-to-point complexity.

**Action:** I established API-led integration principles, ownership boundaries, event or batch patterns where appropriate, security, error handling, reconciliation and monitoring.

**Result:** The talent ecosystem became more connected and maintainable.

**SAP SuccessFactors Succession & Development Example:** Employee/position data from Employee Central and relevant performance or learning information can support talent processes through governed integrations.

**SME Probe:** When would you choose event-driven integration over scheduled integration?

### HR-AGL4-B22-Q06 — Security Architecture

**Interview Question:** How should enterprise security architecture influence Succession & Development design?

### STAR Answer
**Situation:** Succession information contained highly sensitive talent and leadership data.

**Task:** I needed to ensure the architecture protected confidentiality without preventing legitimate talent decisions.

**Action:** I applied data classification, least privilege, role-based access, segregation of duties, lifecycle controls, auditability and secure integration principles.

**Result:** The solution supported talent processes while reducing privacy and security exposure.

**SAP SuccessFactors Succession & Development Example:** Role-based permissions and appropriate access boundaries should distinguish employee, manager, HR, executive and administrative visibility.

**SME Probe:** How would you resolve a conflict between transparency and confidentiality?
