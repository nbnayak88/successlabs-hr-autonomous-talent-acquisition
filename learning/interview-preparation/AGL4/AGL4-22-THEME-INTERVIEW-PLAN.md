# AGL4 — 22-Theme Interview Preparation Plan

## Applied SAP SuccessFactors Succession & Development

**Target:** 22 themes × 20 unique scenario-based interview questions = 440 scenarios

**Answer standard:** STAR — Situation → Task → Action → Result

**Every scenario also requires:** SAP SuccessFactors Succession & Development Example + SME Probe + Architecture Signal + Business Outcome.

---

**Employee → Talent Profile → Potential → Succession → Development → Career Mobility → Workforce Capability → Business Continuity**

# Scenario Construction Standard

Every question must be materially different from the other 19 questions in its theme.

Every answer must contain:

**Situation:** realistic enterprise/talent context.

**Task:** candidate responsibility and measurable objective.

**Action:** specific decisions and actions, including architecture reasoning where appropriate.

**Result:** measurable or clearly evidenced outcome.

**SAP SuccessFactors Succession & Development Example:** product-specific grounding.

**SME Probe:** deeper challenge that tests reasoning.

---

# Architecture Signal

The Action section should reveal relevant dimensions from:

**Business → Process → People → Data → Application → Integration → Security → Experience → Technology → AI → Outcome**

Do not force irrelevant architecture dimensions into a scenario.

---

# Progression

**KNOW → DESIGN → DELIVER → SOLVE → INFLUENCE → TRANSFORM**

The 22 themes deliberately move the learner from product/domain credibility toward enterprise talent-transformation leadership.

# No-Duplicate Gate

Before creating a scenario:

1. Search AGL4 interview-preparation content.
2. Search the wider HR autonomous repository.
3. Check AWF1, ATA2a, ATA2b and other HR academy content where overlap is likely.
4. Reuse/map an existing scenario only when it genuinely belongs to AGL4.
5. Otherwise create a new scenario.
6. Record the distinction.

**AGL4 must not become a renamed copy of Performance & Goals, Employee Central, Recruiting or Onboarding.**
