# AGL4 — Interview Simulator Blueprint

## Applied SAP SuccessFactors Succession & Development

**Purpose:** Convert the AGL4 interview-preparation scenario bank into an adaptive interview-practice and assessment experience.

**Transformation spine:**

**Employee → Talent Profile → Goals & Performance → Potential → Succession → Development → Career Mobility → Workforce Capability → Business Continuity**

**Product posture:** SAP SuccessFactors Succession & Development-first.

**Boundary:** Performance & Goals remains APH3; Compensation remains ARP5; AGL4 focuses on Succession & Development, talent pipelines, career development, and workforce capability.

---

**SELECT → THINK → SPEAK → PROBE → SCORE → REFLECT → REATTEMPT**

## 3. Assessment Rubric — 100 Points

| Dimension | Points |
|---|---:|
| Situation | 10 |
| Task | 10 |
| Action | 20 |
| Result | 15 |
| Architecture | 15 |
| Product | 10 |
| Business Value | 10 |
| SME Probe | 10 |
| **Total** | **100** |

Readiness:
- 90–100: Architect-level
- 80–89: Strong
- 70–79: Good — targeted improvement
- 60–69: Borderline
- <60: Rebuild

Critical security, privacy, data ownership, product-boundary or architecture failures can override the numeric score.

## 8. Adaptive Scenario Selection

| Factor | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Weakness | 35% |
| Theme / progression need | 20% |
| Difficulty fit | 15% |
| Recency / repetition | 10% |
| Diversity | 10% |
| Controlled randomness | 10% |

Rules:
- No immediate scenario repeat.
- Weak capabilities receive priority.
- Strong capabilities are not overtrained.
- Breadth across 22 themes is preserved.
- Difficulty rises only with evidence.
- Weak areas are re-tested later.
