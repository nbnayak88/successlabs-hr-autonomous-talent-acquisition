## HR-ATA2B-B22-Q01 — Enterprise Onboarding Capability

### Interview Question
How would you position SAP SuccessFactors Onboarding as an enterprise capability rather than simply an HR application?

### STAR Answer
**Situation:** The organization viewed onboarding as a collection of HR tasks.
**Task:** I needed to establish its enterprise significance.
**Action:** I mapped onboarding to employee experience, workforce readiness, compliance, identity, IT provisioning, data, payroll dependencies, analytics, and business productivity.
**Result:** Leadership recognized onboarding as a cross-enterprise capability affecting multiple value streams.

### SAP SuccessFactors Onboarding Example
The capability extends from New Hire through Preboarding, Compliance, Employee Setup, Day 1, Integration, and Productivity.

### SME Probe
What makes a capability enterprise-level?
