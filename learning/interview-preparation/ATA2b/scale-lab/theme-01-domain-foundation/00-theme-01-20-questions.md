## HR-ATA2B-B01-Q01 — Onboarding as an Enterprise Capability

### Interview Question
How would you explain SAP SuccessFactors Onboarding as an enterprise capability?

### STAR Answer
**Situation:** A client viewed onboarding as document collection after hiring.
**Task:** I needed to reposition onboarding as a business capability.
**Action:** I mapped the journey from new-hire handoff through preboarding, compliance, tasks, provisioning, Day 1, integration, and productivity.
**Result:** Leadership understood onboarding as an enterprise transition capability rather than an HR checklist.

### SAP SuccessFactors Onboarding Example
SuccessFactors Onboarding orchestrates structured activities for new hires and relevant participants while connecting to the wider HCM ecosystem.

### SME Probe
What makes onboarding an enterprise capability rather than an HR process?

# Theme 01 Completion Standard

A learner completes **ATA2b Theme 01 — Domain Foundation** when they can:

- Explain onboarding as an enterprise capability.
- Describe the complete onboarding lifecycle.
- Distinguish new-hire and employee lifecycle states.
- Define business value and measurable outcomes.
- Model onboarding participants and ownership.
- Architect preboarding and Day-1 readiness.
- Balance global standards with legitimate localization.
- Embed compliance into the process.
- Establish a trusted onboarding data foundation.
- Design experience-led onboarding.
- Define system-of-record and integration boundaries.
- Handle exceptions and security.
- Use analytics for continuous improvement.
- Govern internal hire and rehire variants.
- Establish an operating model and transformation roadmap.

**Quality rule:** Every scenario demonstrates Situation → Task → Action → Result, contains a distinct onboarding architecture decision, uses SAP SuccessFactors Onboarding as the primary example, remains separate from ATA2a Recruiting, avoids duplication with later Onboarding themes, and ends with an SME Probe.
