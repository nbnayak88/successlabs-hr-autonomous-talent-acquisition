## HR-ATA2B-B19-Q01 — Discovering the Client's Real Problem

### Interview Question
During presales, a client asks for a “best-in-class onboarding system.” How would you uncover what they actually need?

### STAR Answer
**Situation:** The client started with a broad technology request rather than a defined business problem.
**Task:** I needed to understand the business outcome behind the request.
**Action:** I explored current-state process, employee experience, cycle time, compliance, manager effort, data quality, integrations, support demand, and strategic priorities before discussing solution features.
**Result:** The opportunity was reframed around measurable onboarding outcomes rather than product functionality alone.

### SAP SuccessFactors Onboarding Example
I would map the journey from New Hire through Day 1 and identify where SuccessFactors Onboarding can address the actual business problems.

### SME Probe
What questions would you ask before demonstrating the product?

# Theme 19 Completion Standard

A learner completes **ATA2b Theme 19 — Presales / Leadership / Decision Making** when they can:

- Discover the real business problem behind a technology request.
- Build outcome-based value propositions and business cases.
- Demonstrate Onboarding through business journeys.
- Lead RFP and presales architecture responses.
- Evaluate make-versus-buy and standard-versus-custom decisions.
- Position solutions objectively against alternatives.
- Estimate implementation complexity with explicit assumptions.
- Identify and communicate presales red flags.
- Lead scope, roadmap, governance, and investment decisions.
- Qualify opportunity and delivery risk.
- Maintain architecture integrity throughout delivery.
- Measure presales quality beyond deal closure.
- Transition solution knowledge effectively into delivery.
- Advise executives decisively while making uncertainty explicit.
- Operate as a trusted transformation leader.

**Quality rule:** Every scenario demonstrates Situation → Task → Action → Result, contains a distinct presales, leadership, or decision-making decision, uses SAP SuccessFactors Onboarding as the primary example, remains separate from ATA2a Recruiting, avoids duplication with Themes 01–18 and later themes, and ends with an SME Probe.
