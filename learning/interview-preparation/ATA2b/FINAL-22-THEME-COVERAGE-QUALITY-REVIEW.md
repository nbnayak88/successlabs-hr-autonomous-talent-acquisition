# 6. Cross-Theme Duplication Control

The themes intentionally address different architectural questions even when the same business object appears.

Examples:

- **Theme 13 — Troubleshooting & RCA:** How do I isolate and prove the root cause?
- **Theme 14 — Scenario Problem Solving:** How do I make the right business/architecture decision under ambiguity?
- **Theme 15 — Risk, Controls & Security:** How do I prevent/control exposure?
- **Theme 16 — Performance & Optimization:** How do I improve throughput, latency, efficiency, and value?
- **Theme 17 — Stakeholder Management:** How do I align people and decision-makers?
- **Theme 18 — Communication & Consulting:** How do I frame, explain, advise, and influence?
- **Theme 19 — Presales / Leadership / Decision Making:** How do I shape investment and enterprise decisions?
- **Theme 20 — Transformation & Roadmap:** How do I sequence future capability?
- **Theme 21 — Innovation & Emerging Technology:** How do I introduce intelligence responsibly?
- **Theme 22 — Enterprise Architecture & Business Value:** How do I connect all architecture dimensions to enterprise value?

Therefore, recurring subjects such as integration, security, data, AI, or employee experience are not automatically duplicates. They are evaluated through different interview lenses.

# 9. Production-Ready Interview Answer Pattern

A candidate should not memorize the 440 answers word-for-word.

The intended learning behavior is:

**Understand the scenario**
→ **Frame the business problem**
→ **Choose the architecture lens**
→ **Answer in STAR**
→ **Give the Onboarding example**
→ **Defend the decision through the SME Probe**

The candidate should be able to adapt the same reasoning to a new enterprise scenario.
