## 6. Simulator Completion

The ATA2b simulator architecture is defined as:

**Select → Think → Speak → Probe → Score → Reflect → Reattempt**

Assessment dimensions:

- Situation — 10
- Task — 10
- Action — 20
- Result — 15
- Architecture — 15
- Product — 10
- Business Value — 10
- SME Probe — 10

**Total = 100**

Readiness:

- 90–100: Architect-level
- 80–89: Strong
- 70–79: Good; targeted improvement
- 60–69: Borderline
- <60: Rebuild

Critical security, privacy, product, architecture, or ownership failures override the numeric score.

## 7. Adaptive Learning

The simulator supports:

- Theme Practice
- Random 20
- Architect Challenge
- Weakness Recovery
- Full Mock

Mastery progression:

**NEW → PRACTICED → DEVELOPING → MASTERED**

Difficulty progression:

**Practitioner → Consultant → Architect → Transformation Architect**

Selection prioritizes:

1. Weakness
2. Theme/progression need
3. Difficulty fit
4. Recency/repetition
5. Diversity
6. Controlled randomness

The design intentionally avoids simple memorization.
